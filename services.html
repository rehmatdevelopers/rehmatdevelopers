🔄 How We Work

Our Proven Process

We keep the process straightforward: understand your requirements, agree on the scope, build the website, review it with you, and prepare it for launch.

01
💬

Discovery & Consultation

We discuss your business, goals, preferred website style, required pages, features, timeline, and budget. Then we summarize the project scope before work begins.

02
🎨

Strategy & Design

We plan the page structure and visual direction using your requirements and feedback. The design approach depends on whether the project is custom-coded or WordPress-based.

03
💻

Development & Testing

We build the agreed pages and functionality, then review the website on common screen sizes and fix issues found during testing. Progress and feedback are shared during the project.

04
🚀

Launch & Ongoing Support

After your approval, we help publish the website using the agreed hosting or deployment method. Any post-launch support or maintenance is provided according to the arrangement made for your project.
