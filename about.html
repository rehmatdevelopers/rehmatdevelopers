📖 Our Story

From a Small Town to the Digital World

Rehmat Developers was born out of a deep passion for technology and a burning desire to make professional web development accessible to businesses of all sizes. Founded by Muhammad Waleed in the heart of Raja Jang, Kasur, Pakistan, our agency carries a name that honors family legacy — "Rehmat" being the name of our founder's beloved father.

What started as a one-man mission to help local businesses establish their online presence has now grown into a full-fledged digital agency serving clients across Pakistan and beyond. We remember the early days — working late nights from a small room, learning new technologies, and delivering projects that made our clients smile. Those humble beginnings shaped our core values: hard work, honesty, and relentless pursuit of excellence.

Today, Rehmat Developers is a team of skilled developers, designers, and strategists who share a common vision — to transform ideas into powerful digital realities. We've worked with startups, small businesses, e-commerce brands, and growing enterprises, delivering over 50 successful projects and counting. Every project we take on is treated as our own, because we believe your success is our success.

We don't just build websites. We build relationships, trust, and digital experiences that stand the test of time. Whether you need a simple landing page or a complex full-stack application, our team is ready to bring your vision to life with precision, creativity, and cutting-edge technology.

📅 Our Journey

The Road So Far

Every milestone tells a story of growth, learning, and determination.

2022

The Spark 💡

Muhammad Waleed began learning web development and building small projects for friends and local businesses in Raja Jang, Kasur. What started as a hobby quickly turned into a passion that would change everything.

2023

First Clients 🤝

Delivered the first 10 professional websites to local businesses. Word-of-mouth referrals started pouring in. The decision was made — Rehmat Developers would become a full-time agency dedicated to digital excellence.

2024

Team Expansion 🚀

Grew the team to include talented frontend developers, backend specialists, and UI/UX designers. Expanded services to include full-stack applications, e-commerce solutions, and digital marketing. Completed 30+ projects.

2025

Going Global 🌍

Launched our premium agency website, started serving international clients, and set ambitious goals for the future. With 50+ projects delivered and a growing reputation for quality, the best is yet to come.
