🔄 Fair & Structured Revision Policy

We want your website to feel right — and we also want timelines and expectations to stay realistic. Every package includes a fixed number of revision rounds during active development. There are no open-ended or unlimited revision promises.

Starter ($299) 2 Revision Rounds
Professional ($799) 3 Revision Rounds
Enterprise ($2,499) 4 Revision Rounds
  • ✓ Within approved scope: Revisions cover refinements inside the originally approved project scope — such as layout polish, content updates, spacing, colors, and typography adjustments.
  • ✓ Quoted separately: Major design overhauls, brand-new features, extra pages, or anything outside the agreed scope is treated as additional work and quoted separately before we proceed.
  • ✓ Consolidated feedback: For each revision round, please send one clear, combined feedback list. Grouping your notes together keeps changes organized, avoids conflicting requests, and helps us deliver faster.
  • ✓ Friendly & transparent: If you are ever unsure whether a request fits inside a revision round or needs a separate quote, just ask — we will guide you honestly before any extra work begins.
