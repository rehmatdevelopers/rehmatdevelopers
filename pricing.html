Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear answers to common questions regarding our web development process, pricing, timelines, and policies.

How much does a website cost?

+

Our pricing is completely fixed and transparent: The Starter package is $299 (up to 5 pages), the Professional package is $799 (up to 10 pages), and the Enterprise / E-Commerce package is $2,499 (unlimited pages/products). You can review all details on our Pricing page.

Are domain and hosting included in the package?

+

No, domain and hosting are separate. Clients purchase their domain name and web hosting directly under their own accounts. This guarantees 100% legal ownership and control over your digital assets forever. We assist you free of charge in setting them up.

How long does it take to build a website?

+

Delivery timelines depend on your package: Starter websites take 1-2 weeks, Professional websites take 2-4 weeks, and Enterprise or E-commerce solutions take 4-8 weeks to complete.

Do you offer refunds if I am not satisfied?

+

We follow a strict 100% Quality & Work Protection Policy. We do not offer post-delivery cash refunds. Instead, we use milestone payments, provide live staging links for real-time testing, and offer unlimited revisions during the active development phase so you are fully satisfied before final delivery.

Do you provide ongoing support after the website is launched?

+

Yes! We provide complimentary post-launch support based on your plan: 30 Days for Starter, 60 Days for Professional, and 90 Days Priority Support for Enterprise.

What information do I need to provide to start?

+

To begin, we need a brief overview of your business, your logo/branding files, preferred colors, specific features required, and text/images for the pages. If you don't have text or images, we can guide you or use professional placeholder content.
